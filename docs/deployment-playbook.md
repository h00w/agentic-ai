# Deployment Playbook

1. Discover: baseline workflow, users, risk, data and business value.
2. Prototype: deterministic reference flow with non-sensitive data.
3. Evaluate: establish golden tasks and quality/cost thresholds.
4. Pilot: prefer read-only tools, human review, tight quotas and telemetry.
5. Gate: security, privacy, operations and business owners review evidence.
6. Limited release: small cohort, canary configuration and rollback ready.
7. Scale: expand only after KPI stability; standardize platform controls.
8. Operate: monitor drift, incidents, access, cost and outcomes.
9. Change: model/tool/prompt/policy changes pass regression gates.
10. Retire: revoke credentials, archive evidence and apply retention rules.

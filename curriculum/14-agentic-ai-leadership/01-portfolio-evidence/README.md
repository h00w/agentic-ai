# Lesson 14.01 — Agentic AI Portfolio Evidence and ROI/TCO

## UNDERSTAND

Prioritize Agentic AI initiatives using measurable value, total cost, risk and evidence maturity.

## BUILD

Create a portfolio evidence record for one use case: baseline process, target outcome, cost drivers, risks and proof level.

## BREAK

Evaluate a use case with an impressive demo but no baseline, owner, adoption signal or production evidence.

## MEASURE

Business value claims include a baseline, target, measurement window, owner and cost/risk assumptions.

## SECURE

Risk and governance effort are part of TCO rather than externalities ignored by the business case.

## SHIP

Complete `artifacts/worksheet.md` and retain the artifact manifest as reviewable evidence for this lesson.

## OPERATE

Review realized value, adoption, incident cost, inference/tool spend and maintenance load.

## GOVERN

Use evidence maturity to sequence investment without treating prototype success as production validation.

# Lesson 01.01 — Agent or Workflow Decision Record

## UNDERSTAND

Decide whether a use case needs an agent, a deterministic workflow, or a hybrid.

## BUILD

Complete the decision worksheet for one real use case. Separate uncertainty, adaptive planning and tool choice from steps that are fully specifiable.

## BREAK

Force an agent into a task with a complete fixed mapping and identify the unnecessary variance, cost and attack surface.

## MEASURE

Decision is acceptable when the proposed autonomy is justified by uncertainty or adaptive sequencing and deterministic steps remain outside the model.

## SECURE

Treat side effects, permissions and irreversible actions as reasons to reduce autonomy or introduce approval.

## SHIP

Complete `artifacts/worksheet.md` and retain the artifact manifest as reviewable evidence for this lesson.

## OPERATE

Track escalation rate, unnecessary model calls, failed actions and operator overrides.

## GOVERN

Record the decision owner, review date and the evidence that would justify increasing or decreasing autonomy.

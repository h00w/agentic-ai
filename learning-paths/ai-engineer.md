# AI/ML Engineer Learning Path

**Suggested duration:** 8 weeks

Prioritize architecture, evaluation, observability, security, and production reliability.

## Sequence
1. [LLM and Agent Foundations](../curriculum/03-llm-agent-foundations/README.md)
2. [Single-Agent Engineering](../curriculum/04-single-agent-engineering/README.md)
3. [RAG, Knowledge and Memory](../curriculum/06-rag-memory-knowledge/README.md)
4. [Multi-Agent Systems](../curriculum/07-multi-agent-systems/README.md)
5. [Agent Evaluation](../curriculum/08-agent-evaluation/README.md)
6. [Observability and Operations](../curriculum/11-observability/README.md)
7. [Security and Safety](../curriculum/09-agent-security/README.md)
8. [Production Agent Engineering](../curriculum/10-production-engineering/README.md)
9. [Governance, Risk and Scaling](../curriculum/12-governance-scaling/README.md)
10. [Enterprise Agentic AI Architecture](../curriculum/13-enterprise-architecture/README.md)

## Completion evidence
For every stage, retain code, tests, architecture, evaluation evidence, a risk note, and a short reflection on what you would change for production. Finish with the Responsible Enterprise Research Agent capstone.
